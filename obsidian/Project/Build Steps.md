# Build Steps

The Build List splits the work into **six steps**. The clock starts on the day the pack was received (22 Sep).

| Step | What happens | Due (working days) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations: the engine's home, databases and basic setup | 7 (≈ 30 Sep) |
| 2 | Receiving app data, the interest score on real data, serving lists | 12 (≈ 7 Oct) |
| 3 | Finding candidate posts, product and promoter scores | 17 (≈ 14 Oct) |
| 4 | Ranking the feed, fraud rules, speed test, checks | 22 (≈ 21 Oct) |
| 5 | Final acceptance checks and monitoring | 27 (≈ 28 Oct) |
| 6 | Clear test data and go live | before launch |

## Good to know
- **No waiting on the calendar.** A task can start as soon as whatever it waits on is done.
- **Nobody signs things off.** Each task has a check, and if the check passes, the task is done.
- **Tell Kala early if something won't fit.** If a task can't finish inside its step, I must tell her in writing within 5 working days of receiving the pack (≈ 28 Sep). Otherwise it counts as agreed.
